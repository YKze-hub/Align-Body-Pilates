The equipment

Five machines, one system.

Every apparatus in the studio is BASI Systems — the classical Pilates lineage, built for precision, resistance, and control.

01

Reformer

The core apparatus of the method. A sliding carriage and adjustable springs build full-body strength, control, and alignment through resistance.

Full body
02

Cadillac / Trapeze Table

Bars, straps, and springs suspended over a table — used for assisted stretching, suspension work, and rehab-focused movement.

Mobility & rehab
03

F2 Arm Chair

A compact resistance station built for the arms, shoulders, and core — sharpening stability in a small footprint.

Upper body
04

Chair

A single spring-loaded step that trains balance, strength, and functional movement in a standing position.

Balance & strength
05

Spine Corrector

A curved barrel that supports spinal articulation — opening the chest, lengthening the spine, and refining posture.

Posture & flexibility
